Account & Data Deletion Request

If you wish to delete your account and associated data for MindQuiz: Brain Challenge, you can request full data removal at any time.

Steps to Request Account Deletion:

  1. Send an email to our support team using the button below or directly via email.
  2. Use the subject line: Account Deletion Request - MindQuiz.
  3. Provide your registered Google Sign-In email address in the body of the message.

Data That Will Be Deleted:

  • Profile information (Name, Email address, Profile picture URL).
  • App activity history (Quiz scores, progress, and performance data).
  • Unique User IDs and associated database records.

Data Retention Period:

Upon receiving your request, your account and all associated personal data will be permanently deleted from our Firebase servers within 7 days.
